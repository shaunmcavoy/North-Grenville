Why people choose the Cabin

The Cabin is the bigger modern outdoor sauna, with facing benches for four, a full-height glass front and a weatherproof black composite exterior built to stand in the garden all year. It suits a bigger yard and a household that wants room to spread out.

Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Cabin may be perfect for you if…

  • It's a family of four
  • You want a modern black sauna that matches a contemporary home
  • You want low maintenance outside
  • You like music while you sit
  • You'll use it all year

Blaine's honest take

It needs a 240V circuit and a firm, level base. If a wood-burning stove matters to you, the Cube and Barrel ranges offer one.

How it heats

Traditional heat.

A heater warms a bed of stones and the air around you to a real, enveloping heat. Pour water on the stones for a burst of steam. This is the classic Scandinavian sauna, and it takes about 30 to 45 minutes to heat up.

Modern black cabins in weatherproof, low-maintenance composite, glass-fronted and wired for sound.

Pair it with your hot tub for a hot and cold routine at home. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before using a sauna.
