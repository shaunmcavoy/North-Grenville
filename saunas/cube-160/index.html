Why people choose the Cube 160

The Cube 160 is the sculptural, glass-fronted outdoor sauna, sized for two or three. A full double-glazed front and a half panoramic window let you watch the snow while you sit in real heat, and the 40 mm thermo-spruce frame and shingled roof are made for every season.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Cube 160 may be perfect for you if…

  • It's two or three of you
  • You want to look out at the yard while you sit
  • You'll use it all winter
  • You like the idea of a wood-burning stove
  • You want it to look beautiful from the house

Blaine's honest take

Like every outdoor sauna it needs a firm, level base, and the electric heater needs a 240V circuit. A wood-burning stove avoids the circuit but means building a fire each time.

How it heats

Traditional heat.

A heater warms a bed of stones and the air around you to a real, enveloping heat. Pour water on the stones for a burst of steam. This is the classic Scandinavian sauna, and it takes about 30 to 45 minutes to heat up.

Glass-fronted garden saunas in heat-treated spruce, with a shingled roof made for every season.

Pair it with your hot tub for a hot and cold routine at home. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before using a sauna.
