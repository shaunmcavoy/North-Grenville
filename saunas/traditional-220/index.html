Why people choose the Traditional 220

The Traditional 220 is the largest of our indoor traditional saunas, with room for six on two bench heights. It's the one for big families, home gyms and people who like to share the heat with friends.

Is it the one?

The Traditional 220 may be perfect for you if…

  • You want room for six
  • You're building it into a home gym or rec room
  • You have friends over for sauna nights
  • You want real heat and steam
  • You have about 7 ft 3 in by 7 ft 1 in of space

Blaine's honest take

A bigger room takes longer to heat and needs a 240V circuit. If it's usually just two or three of you, the 160 or the 210 will heat up faster.

How it heats

Traditional heat.

A traditional sauna heats the whole room and gets to a very high heat. Pour water on the hot rocks to raise the humidity, and the room feels hotter still. This is the classic Scandinavian sauna, it takes about 30 to 45 minutes to heat up, and all of our outdoor saunas are traditional.

Indoor traditional saunas in knot-free thermo-aspen, with glass fronts and two bench heights.

Pair it with your hot tub for a hot and cold routine at home. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before using a sauna.
