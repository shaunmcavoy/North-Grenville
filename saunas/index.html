The first choice

Traditional or infrared?

Both give you twenty quiet minutes of warmth. The difference is how they heat, and that decides how it feels.

Traditional

A traditional sauna heats the whole room and gets to a very high heat. Pour water on the hot rocks to raise the humidity, and the room feels hotter still. This is the classic Scandinavian sauna, it takes about 30 to 45 minutes to heat up, and all of our outdoor saunas are traditional.

Infrared

Infrared warms your body directly instead of heating the air around you. That's why it feels deeply warm at a much lower temperature, around 45 to 60°C, and many people find it easier to relax and stay in longer. It's ready in minutes, with no rocks, water or steam to manage.

Our outdoor saunas are the Cube, Barrel and Cabin ranges, and they are all traditional. The Cube and Barrel can also take a wood-burning stove.
