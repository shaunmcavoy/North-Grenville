Why people choose the Palma

83 jets in a 6 ft 7 in tub is a lot of massage for the space. The big 5 inch jets give broad, deep pressure across your back, and the dozens of small jets work into the tighter spots. People buy the Palma when they want real hydrotherapy at a Deluxe price.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Palma may be perfect for you if…

  • Your back or shoulders are sore at the end of the day
  • You want deep massage without paying for a bigger line
  • You'd like a full lounger plus five seats
  • Your yard fits a 6 ft 7 in square
  • You want different seats for different kinds of massage

Blaine's honest take

If you want even more power and more water around you, the Tokyo and the Maximus in the Premium line go further. For most sore backs, though, the Palma is plenty.

How the seats work

5 seats and 1 lounger.

One full-size lounger and five sculpted seats, with 7 large, 14 medium and 62 small jets spread between them.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Your lounger

Lie back and the jets run from your shoulders down to your feet. Most people try it first.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
