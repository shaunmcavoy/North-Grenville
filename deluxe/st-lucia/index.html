Why people choose the St Lucia

The St Lucia is for people who sit up and talk. Four seats and no lounger means nobody is stretched out across the tub and everyone gets a proper seat. It plugs into a regular outlet, it has Wi-Fi so you can check on it from your phone, and it comes ready for a heat pump if you want to add one later.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The St Lucia may be perfect for you if…

  • You soak to talk, more than to lie back
  • It's two to four people most nights
  • You like checking and adjusting things from your phone
  • You might heat it with a heat pump down the road
  • You want to plug in and go, with no electrician

Blaine's honest take

This tub is built for conversation more than deep massage. If a sore back is the real reason you're shopping, the Palma or the Tahiti will do more for you.

How the seats work

4 seats, no lounger.

Four upright seats, each with its own jet layout, so you can trade spots and get a different massage.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Upright seats

Everyone sits up at the same height, which makes it easier to talk and to fit more people in.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
