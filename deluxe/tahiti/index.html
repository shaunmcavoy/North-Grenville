Why people choose the Tahiti

People choose the Tahiti because it fits where bigger tubs don't: a small deck, a townhouse patio, a tucked-away corner of the yard. It still gives you a full lounger to stretch out in, and it plugs into a regular outdoor outlet, so you can be soaking the weekend it arrives.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Tahiti may be perfect for you if…

  • It's mostly the two of you, with room for one more
  • You want to lie back and stretch out, not just sit
  • Your space is tight, about 7 ft by 5 ft 5 in
  • You'd rather not hire an electrician to get started
  • It's your first hot tub and you want to keep it simple

Blaine's honest take

On a regular 120V outlet the heater is small, so it warms up slowly after you add cold water. If you'll be in it every night through January, ask me about wiring it for 240V. The same tub goes from a 1 kW to a 4 kW heater.

How the seats work

2 seats and 1 lounger.

Two seats and one lounger, each with its own mix of jets, so every spot feels a little different.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Your lounger

Lie back and the jets run from your shoulders down to your feet. Most people try it first.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
