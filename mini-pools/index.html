Mini pools · Platinum Spas

A pool built into your backyard.

Mini pools are made to be fully sunk into the ground. There's no cabinet and no siding, because they're designed to be built seamlessly into your landscaping, with your patio stone, decking or lawn running right to the edge. You get the look of an in-ground pool in a size that fits a yard a full pool never would, heated for year-round use, with a current to swim against.

The first choice

River jet or turbine?

Every mini pool gives you a current to swim against. The difference is how it's made, and that decides how it feels.

River jet

Strong jets push a smooth, adjustable current you swim against. Good for light swimming, resistance work and hydrotherapy, at a lower price than the turbine.

The Atlas, The Chronos, The Hades
16-speed turbine

A belt-driven turbine pulls a wide, smooth current across the whole lane, with no bubbles or turbulence. Sixteen speeds run from gentle to hard training. It's the choice for serious swimmers.

The Atlas Pro, The Chronos Pro, The Hades Pro, The Artemis Pro

Every mini pool comes in both. Then pick the layout: a corner step (Atlas), a full-width step (Chronos), a completely open lane (Hades), or two loungers (Artemis Pro).
