Why people choose the Artemis Pro

The Artemis Pro is for people who want to swim and relax in the same pool. It has a flat swim area in front of a 16-speed turbine, plus two full-body loungers and a central step. Train hard, then lie back. It's fully in-ground with a clean, architectural finish.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Artemis Pro may be perfect for you if…

  • You want to swim and relax in one pool
  • You'd love two loungers to stretch out on
  • You want a smooth turbine current to train with
  • You want a central step that's easy to use
  • You want a pool that looks designed into your yard

Blaine's honest take

The loungers take up some of the lane, so serious lap swimmers will prefer the Atlas Pro or the Hades Pro. The turbine needs two circuits, 40A and 50A.

How it works

16-speed turbine current.

A belt-driven turbine pulls a wide, smooth current across the whole lane, with no bubbles or turbulence. Sixteen speeds run from gentle to hard training. It's the choice for serious swimmers.

Two full-body loungers and a central access step, with a flat swim area in front of the turbine.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
