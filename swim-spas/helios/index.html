Why people choose the Helios

The Helios is two tubs in one shell. The swim end and the hot tub end are fully separate, each with its own heater, so you can keep the swim water cooler for exercise and the spa end hot for a soak. The spa end seats five adults, with a lounger.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Helios may be perfect for you if…

  • You want to swim cool and soak hot
  • Your family wants a full hot tub, not just a couple of seats
  • You'd like a lounger at the spa end
  • You want resistance workouts and aquatic exercise
  • You have room for a tub about 19 ft long

Blaine's honest take

Two bodies of water means two heaters and two power supplies. It's the right choice if you'll use both ends, and more than you need if you mostly swim.

How it works

River jet current.

Strong jets push a current you swim against. They double as massage jets for the cool-down, and they suit people who want a swim and a soak.

Four seats and a lounger in a separate hot tub end, with 50 massage jets of every size.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
