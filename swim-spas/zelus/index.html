Why people choose the Zelus

The Zelus gives you a longer swim area, at about 18 ft 8 in overall, with three river jets for a focused current. It's made for resistance workouts and aquatic exercise, and the spa end has two seats and a lounger for afterwards. It's one of the best values in a full-length swim spa.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Zelus may be perfect for you if…

  • Swimming or aquatic exercise is the main reason you're buying
  • You want a longer lane than the compact models
  • You'd like a lounger to stretch out in after a workout
  • It's mostly one to three people
  • You want full length at the best value

How it works

River jet current.

Strong jets push a current you swim against. They double as massage jets for the cool-down, and they suit people who want a swim and a soak.

Two seats and a lounger at the spa end, with large jets for tired shoulders and legs.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
