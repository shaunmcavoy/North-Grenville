The first choice

River jet or turbine?

Every swim spa gives you a current to swim against. The difference is how it's made, and that decides how it feels.

River jet

Strong jets push a current you swim against. They double as massage jets for the cool-down, and they suit people who want a swim and a soak.

The Eros, The Helios, The Titan, The Zelus
16-speed turbine

A belt-driven turbine pulls a wide, smooth current across the whole lane, with no bubbles or turbulence. Sixteen speeds run from gentle to hard training. It's the choice for serious swimmers.

The Eros Pro, The Helios Pro, The Zelus Pro

The Helios and Helios Pro also split into two separate tubs, so the swim end and the hot tub end can sit at different temperatures.
