Why people choose the Titan

The Titan is the swim spa for a crowd. It has seats for nine and a lounger, so it works like a very big hot tub, and the river jets give you a current for swimming, water walking and low-impact exercise. At 48 inches tall it's shallower and shorter than our other swim spas.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Titan may be perfect for you if…

  • You want a big family tub more than a lap pool
  • You host and want room for up to ten
  • You want low-impact exercise like water walking
  • You'd like a swim current for the kids to play in
  • A shorter, shallower swim spa suits your yard

Blaine's honest take

It's built for soaking and play first. If swimming laps is the main goal, the Zelus or the Zelus Pro give you a longer, deeper swim lane.

How it works

River jet current.

Strong jets push a current you swim against. They double as massage jets for the cool-down, and they suit people who want a swim and a soak.

Nine seats and a lounger around the swim area, with jets aimed at the back and shoulders for a proper massage.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
