Why people choose the Helios Pro

The Helios Pro keeps everything people love about the Helios, a fully separate hot tub end with its own temperature, and swaps the river jets for a 16-speed turbine. You can swim, aqua-jog or resistance-train against a smooth current, then step over into a hot tub that seats five.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Helios Pro may be perfect for you if…

  • You swim seriously and want a smooth, even current
  • You want to control the swim and spa temperatures separately
  • Your family wants a real hot tub with a lounger
  • You want sixteen speeds, from rehab to hard training
  • You have room for a tub about 19 ft long

Blaine's honest take

This is our most complete swim spa, and it needs the power and the space to match. If you don't need separate temperatures, the Zelus Pro gives you the turbine in a single body of water.

How it works

16-speed turbine current.

A belt-driven turbine pulls a wide, smooth current across the whole lane, with no bubbles or turbulence. Sixteen speeds run from gentle to hard training. It's the choice for serious swimmers.

Four seats and a lounger in a separate hot tub end, with massage jets for recovery after a swim.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
