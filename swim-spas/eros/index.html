Why people choose the Eros

The Eros is one of the most compact swim spas we carry, at just under 15 ft long, and there's still plenty of room to swim or play. Three river jets give you a current to swim against, and the two seats at the end give you a real hot tub soak afterwards.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Eros may be perfect for you if…

  • You want to swim at home but your yard is on the small side
  • It's mostly one or two of you swimming
  • You want a swim and a soak, not a training pool
  • The kids would love a pool they can use 12 months a year
  • You want the best value way into a swim spa

Blaine's honest take

It's one body of water, so the swim end and the seats are the same temperature. If you want a cooler swim and a hotter soak at the same time, look at the Helios.

How it works

River jet current.

Strong jets push a current you swim against. They double as massage jets for the cool-down, and they suit people who want a swim and a soak.

Two hot tub seats at one end, with large massage jets for your back and shoulders after a swim.

The best way to know is to see it in person. Text Blaine and he'll talk you through what fits your yard and how you'll use it.
