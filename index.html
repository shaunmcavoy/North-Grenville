Why we exist

Fifteen years on the sales floor. One thing he couldn't keep selling.

Blaine McAvoy has sold hot tubs for more than fifteen years. Working for other dealers, he kept seeing the same thing: customers steered away from the best tub for them, because the real money came later, from selling chemicals for years after the sale.

The result was owners fighting their water, soaking in harsh chemicals, and treating their tub like a chore. That's the opposite of what a hot tub is for.

So he opened North Grenville Hot Tubs to do it the other way. A small warehouse, the right tub for your family, the cleanest and simplest water care we can find, and fair prices because we keep our overhead low.

A hot tub is where you put the phone down, turn off the news, and talk to the people you love.

Why we partner with Platinum Spas

Built for the person in the water, not the dealer on the floor.

Plenty of hot tubs look beautiful in a showroom. The difference is in the parts you never see: the plumbing, the pumps, the heater and the sanitation system. That's where a lot of brands quietly save money, because you can't see it in the store. Platinum Spas builds it the other way around, for the family that will sit in that tub every night for years.

Bigger plumbing, so more water reaches every jet.

Think about the difference between a tiny hotel shower head and a big rainfall shower. The small one gives you thin, sharp jets that sting more than they soothe. The big one gives you a full, heavy flow of water that wraps around you. A lot of hot tubs are built like the first one: narrow plumbing behind the jets, so the water comes out fast, thin and prickly.

Platinum runs larger plumbing to every jet in every tub they build. More water moves over a bigger area, and that's what a real hydrotherapy massage feels like: broad, deep and relaxing, the kind that lets your shoulders finally drop.

You have to feel it to believe it.

Quality where you can't see it.

The pumps, the heater and the water care system are what decide how a tub feels in year five, not just on day one. That's where Platinum puts the money.

Clean water, built in.

Every Platinum tub has ozone sanitation working every filter cycle, so you need less chlorine and the water stays soft on your skin.

Solid from the ground up.

A welded stainless steel frame on Platinum's insulated Ever Base, so the tub sits on a solid, insulated footing through Ontario winters.

I learned the hard way, back in my twenties, that a hot tub can look great and still be built with cheap pumps, thin plumbing and a heater that won't last. I won't sell one of those to my neighbours. Platinum builds the tub I'd want in my own backyard.

The Hot Tub Diet

Put your hot tub on a diet.

Our water in North Grenville is hard. Heat pulls those minerals out as scale, and body oils feed biofilm inside the pipes. Most dealers answer that with more chemicals. We'd rather sell you fewer. Every tub we sell gets set up with O-Care, a weekly mineral treatment that stops buildup at the source, so the water stays soft and the tub lasts longer.

Less junk in. More years out.

Register your O-Care

Own one of our tubs? Register with Blaine as your advisor and O-Care is delivered free to your door, with a saving on every box. You'll never run out halfway through the season, and you can pause or cancel any time.

The junk-food diet

  • Cloudy water, add more chemicals
  • Smell, shock it again
  • Scale on the heater, jets that clog
  • An owner who stops using the tub

The clean diet

  • Two capfuls of O-Care a week
  • Less sanitizer needed
  • Softer water on skin, kids included
  • A tub that stays young for years
