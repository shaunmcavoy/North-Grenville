How the seats work

6 seats, all upright.

Six sculpted seats, a cup holder built into the seating, and a jet mix from one extra-large jet down to 24 small ones.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Upright seats

Everyone sits up at the same height, face to face, with room to move around. It's the layout people pick when the tub is for talking, family nights and having people over.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
