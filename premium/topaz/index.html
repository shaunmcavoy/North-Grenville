Why people choose the Topaz

The Topaz is the smallest tub in the Premium line, and it puts two adult loungers side by side. At just over 5 ft wide it fits narrow spaces, and with two pumps and 64 jets it doesn't give up any massage for its size.

Blaine McAvoy
Owner, North Grenville Hot Tubs

Is it the one?

The Topaz may be perfect for you if…

  • It's the two of you, and you both want the lounger
  • The kids have grown up and moved out
  • Your space is long and narrow
  • You want strong massage in a small tub
  • Your soak is your quiet time together

Blaine's honest take

With only one regular seat it isn't a tub for entertaining. If you'll have guests over, the Barcelona keeps the two loungers and adds three seats.

How the seats work

1 seat and 2 loungers.

Two adult-sized loungers and one seat, with sculpted headrests and an illuminated waterfall.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Your lounger

Lie back and the jets run from your shoulders down to your feet. Most people try it first.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
