Why people choose the Santorini

The Santorini is the middle of the road in the best way. It has a lounger for solo time and five seats for family time, with 52 jets spread so every seat gets massage where it's needed. At 6 ft 11 in square it fits more yards than the bigger Premium tubs.

Is it the one?

The Santorini may be perfect for you if…

  • You're a family of four to six
  • Some of you want massage and some just want to soak
  • You'd like a lounger for quiet nights alone
  • A 7 ft 4 in tub is a bit big for your space
  • You want clean water with ozone and good filtration

How the seats work

5 seats and 1 lounger.

Five seats and one lounger, with sculpted headrests, drink holders and illuminated waterfalls.

Rotating jets

They spin as they push, for a firmer, deeper massage. These are the ones people want on sore backs and shoulders.

Directional jets

A steady, gentler stream. Good for tired legs, arms and feet, or when you just want to unwind.

Your lounger

Lie back and the jets run from your shoulders down to your feet. Most people try it first.

The best way to know is to sit in it. Come to the warehouse by appointment and try the seats before you decide.
